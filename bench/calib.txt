How does a refrigerator keep food cold? It does not make cold; it moves heat. A liquid called a refrigerant is pumped through a loop of pipes. Inside the fridge, the refrigerant evaporates at low pressure, and evaporating takes heat from its surroundings, so the air inside cools down. A compressor then squeezes the vapour, which makes it hot, and pushes it through coils on the back of the fridge, where it gives that heat to the kitchen and turns back into a liquid. The cycle repeats for as long as the motor runs.

def word_counts(text):
    """Count how often each word appears, ignoring case and punctuation."""
    counts = {}
    for raw in text.split():
        word = raw.strip(".,!?;:\"'()").lower()
        if word:
            counts[word] = counts.get(word, 0) + 1
    return sorted(counts.items(), key=lambda kv: kv[1], reverse=True)

fn fibonacci(n: u64) -> u64 {
    let (mut a, mut b) = (0, 1);
    for _ in 0..n {
        (a, b) = (b, a + b);
    }
    a
}

In economics, opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative you give up when you make a choice. If you spend an evening studying instead of working a paid shift, the opportunity cost of studying includes the wages you did not earn. The idea is simple, but it explains a great deal: why prices change, why people specialise, and why free things are rarely free.
